# 3.14 Studio

A study app with a Socratic AI coach for the Odyssey 3.14 approach to business model innovation.

**Open the app:** https://jeannezipprich-bot.github.io/314-studio/ (GitHub Pages, no AI) · [claude.ai version with the AI coach](https://claude.ai/artifact/4dvNsaEYAP51sZMRWBN4ma)

- **Learn**: the three pillars (value proposition, value architecture, contributions), the three critical prerequisites, the three stages, and an interactive board of the 14 directions with the course questions, examples and diagrams.
- **Analyse**: name a company; the AI maps it onto the three pillars, the directions it uses, the orthodoxies it challenged and directions it has not explored. Students can predict the directions first and compare.
- **Workshop**: a four-step workbook (dominant model, orthodoxies, ideas by direction with their effect on contributions, attractiveness × feasibility matrix and test plan). Each idea records its origin and the app generates an AI-use appendix. Saved in the browser.
- **Practice**: match 14 real course cases to the right direction.

The AI coach and the Analyse tab run in the claude.ai version for anyone signed in to Claude: https://claude.ai/artifact/4dvNsaEYAP51sZMRWBN4ma. This GitHub copy runs without AI; everything else works.

Content follows the course videos M2V1–M2V16 and *Reinvent your Business Model with the Odyssey 3.14 approach* (Lehmann-Ortega & Musikas, Dunod, 2nd ed.). Diagrams are redrawn; no course images or logos are embedded.

A student project built for the Odyssey 3.14 course at HEC Paris. Not an official HEC Paris tool.
